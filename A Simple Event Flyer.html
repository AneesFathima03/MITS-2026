About the Event

The Beginner Coding Workshop is designed for students who are new to web development and want to learn the basics of HTML in a simple and friendly environment. This session is useful because it helps beginners understand webpage structure, headings, paragraphs, lists, links, and special characters.

This workshop is ideal for first-year students, beginners, and anyone who wants to improve their digital skills.

What to Bring

  • Notebook and pen
  • Good mood
